# Fluid balance

Fluid balance describes intake, distribution, and loss. A deficit can reduce circulating volume.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Intake | Water and electrolytes received |
| Output | Measured or estimated loss |

**Dehydration** is a clinical state; a single sign does not establish its cause. Sources: [WHO public guidance](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/drinking-water). Creator: Osmium sample authors; license: CC0-1.0; original educational text.
