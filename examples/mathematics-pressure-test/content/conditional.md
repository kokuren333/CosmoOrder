# Conditional probability

When $P(B)>0$,

$$
P(A \mid B)=\frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}
$$

For a two-state vector, $\begin{bmatrix}P(A)\\P(A^c)\end{bmatrix}$, entries sum to 1. Superscripts $x^2$, subscripts $a_i$, Greek symbols $\alpha, \beta$, and a long identity $P(A \cap B)=P(A \mid B)P(B)$ are common notation.
